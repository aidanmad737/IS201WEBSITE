Printadontics

Software I co-founded for dentists and orthodontists. It lets a practice design and 3D-print aligners, trays, retainers, and snore guards in-office instead of sending the work to an outside dental lab. Features include AI tray generation from patient scans, an attachment library, and print-ready export.
